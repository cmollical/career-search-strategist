## Result Review Rule

For each promising role:

1. Check whether it advances the intended move.
2. Verify the opening on the employer's official careers site.
3. Score it as pursue, explore, or pass.
4. Apply through the official employer site when available.
5. Tailor the resume from verified evidence only.
6. Record the next action in `SEARCH_STATE.md`.
