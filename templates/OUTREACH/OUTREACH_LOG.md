## Drafting Notes

Use:

**Context → Relevance → Reason → Small ask**

Match the relationship and the user's natural voice.

Avoid:

- generic praise
- long autobiography
- immediate cold referral requests
- repeated "just checking in" follow-ups
- manufactured familiarity
