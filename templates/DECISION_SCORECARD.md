## Rating Scale

**5:** Excellent  
**4:** Strong  
**3:** Acceptable or unclear  
**2:** Weak  
**1:** Poor  

## Interpretation

- 85–100: high-priority pursuit
- 75–84: strong opportunity, resolve key questions
- 65–74: selective pursuit if upside is compelling
- below 65: likely pass unless important information is missing

A hard-constraint failure can override the score.
