# Outreach and Networking

## Purpose

Networking should create access, information, and credibility.

It should not feel like mass prospecting.

## Contact priority

Prioritize:

1. warm former colleagues and managers
2. second-degree connections with a credible introduction path
3. leaders one level above target roles
4. peers in target roles
5. adjacent leaders
6. hiring managers for high-fit live openings
7. cold contacts with a specific reason for outreach

## Message formula

Use:

**Context → Relevance → Reason → Small ask**

Example structure:

> We worked together on [context]. I've been exploring [direction], and your move into [relevant area] caught my attention. I'd love to hear how you're thinking about [specific topic]. Would you be open to a quick conversation sometime in the next couple of weeks?

Do not copy this mechanically. Match the user's voice and relationship.

## Hiring-manager outreach

A hiring-manager note should make the relevance obvious quickly.

Useful ingredients:

- applied or planning to apply
- one sentence on fit
- one or two unusually relevant proof points
- a low-pressure close

Do not send a miniature cover letter.

## Networking call

A useful balance:

- 40% their role/team/company
- 30% shared problems and perspective
- 20% candidate proof
- 10% direction and next step

## Referral asks

A referral is easier to request when the other person has enough context to feel comfortable attaching their reputation.

For weak relationships, ask for perspective first.

For strong relationships, a direct referral request can be appropriate.

## Follow-up

Follow up when there is a reason:

- new role posted
- application submitted
- useful article or insight
- mutual contact
- interview progress
- meaningful update

Avoid repeated "just checking in" messages.

## Relationship ledger

Track:

- person
- company
- role
- relationship strength
- last contact
- context
- possible relevance
- next action
- next-action date

The purpose is not CRM theater. Track only what helps maintain real relationships and execute the search.
